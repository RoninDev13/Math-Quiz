# Math Quiz Game (C++)

A dynamic interactive command-line **Math Quiz** game written in C++. Test your arithmetic skills across customizable difficulty levels, choose specific mathematical operations, receive real-time visual feedback, and track your overall quiz results.

---

# 🎮 How to Play

### Step 1: Set Number of Questions
When launched, enter the total number of math questions you would like to solve during your quiz session.

### Step 2: Choose Difficulty Level
Select your preferred number range difficulty:
* `[1]` **Easy:** Numbers from 1 to 10
* `[2]` **Med:** Numbers from 11 to 50
* `[3]` **Hard:** Numbers from 51 to 100
* `[4]` **Mix:** Random mix of all ranges (1 to 100)

### Step 3: Choose Operation Type
Select the arithmetic operation you want to practice:
* `[1]` ➕ **Add** | `[2]` ➖ **Sub** | `[3]` ✖️ **Mul** | `[4]` ➗ **Div** | `[5]` 🔀 **Mix**

### Step 4: Answer Questions & View Feedback
Type your answer for each generated question and press Enter. The outcome displays immediately:
* 🟩 **Green Screen:** Correct answer.
* 🟥 **Red Screen + Audio Beep:** Incorrect answer, along with the reveal of the correct answer.

### Step 5: Endgame Summary
Once all questions are answered, the console displays a complete scorecard detailing:
* **Final Result:** Pass or Fail
* **Questions Level & Operation Type**
* **Total Right Answers & Total Wrong Answers**

### Step 6: Play Again or Exit
* Enter `1` to **Restart** a fresh quiz session.
* Enter `2` to **Exit** the program.
